Pixel Purr Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date] · Published by [your name or company]

The short version. Pixel Purr has no accounts and we don't run a server. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Apple or Google with help from RevenueCat. Those companies receive some device data to do their job, as explained below. You can change your ad choices at any time in the game's settings.

What stays on your device

Your levels, coins, lives, boosters, sticker book and settings are saved only on your phone or tablet. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

We, the makers of Pixel Purr, don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we don't sell personal data. The game doesn't ask you to sign in.

Services that receive data

ServiceWhyWhat it receives
Google AdMob To show ads, including optional reward videos, and to measure them Device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), advertising ID when you allow it, ad interactions and crash diagnostics. See how Google uses data from partner apps.
RevenueCat To confirm and restore in-app purchases A random app user ID created on your device, your purchase history, and device and app information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
Apple App Store / Google Play To process payments Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card.

Your ad choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the game may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, ads are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads or the Cosy Bundle turns off the ads that play between levels. Reward videos stay optional.

Children

Pixel Purr is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance. If you believe a child has used the game and data was shared with the services above, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best made to Google or RevenueCat using the links above, but you can always write to us and we'll point you in the right direction or forward the request.

Security and retention

Data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections. Each service keeps data according to its own policy. Game data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
